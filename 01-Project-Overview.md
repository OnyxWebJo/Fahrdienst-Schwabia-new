\---

\# Project Name

Fahrdienst Schwabia

Premium Airport Transfer & Chauffeur Service Website

Version 1.0

\---

\# Project Purpose

The objective is to develop a premium, multilingual, SEO-first airport transfer website that represents Fahrdienst Schwabia as one of the leading airport transportation companies in Bavaria.

The website should focus on converting visitors into customers through a modern interface, an easy booking process, excellent performance, and strong visibility on Google Search, Google Maps, AI Search Engines, and Local Search.

This is not just a company website.

It is a marketing platform, booking platform, and content platform designed to continuously generate new customers.

\---

\# Business Goals

The website should help the company achieve the following:

• Increase online bookings

• Increase phone calls

• Increase WhatsApp bookings

• Increase Google rankings

• Dominate airport transfer searches in Augsburg

• Improve trust and credibility

• Reduce manual booking work

• Allow the owner to manage prices without a developer

• Support future expansion to additional cities and airports
