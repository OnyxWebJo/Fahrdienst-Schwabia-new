\# 1. SEO Strategy Purpose

The website must be built with SEO as a core requirement.

SEO is not an additional feature after development.

Every page, component, image, URL, and content block must be created with ranking performance in mind.

The goal:

Generate organic traffic from customers searching for airport transportation services in Augsburg and surrounding areas.

\---

\# 2. SEO Objectives

Main objectives:

\- Rank for airport transfer keywords

\- Rank in Google Maps

\- Appear in Google AI Overview

\- Appear in ChatGPT search results

\- Appear in Gemini results

\- Improve local visibility

\- Increase direct bookings
