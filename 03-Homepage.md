\# Homepage Purpose

The homepage is the most important page of the website.

Its goals:

\- Convert visitors into bookings

\- Rank for airport transfer keywords

\- Build trust immediately

\- Explain services quickly

\- Generate phone calls

\- Generate WhatsApp inquiries

\- Support Google Search, AI Search, and Local SEO

The homepage must be designed as a \*\*conversion landing page\*\*, not only a company introduction page.
