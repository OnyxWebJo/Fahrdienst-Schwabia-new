\# 1. Admin Dashboard Purpose

The admin dashboard is a private management system that allows the owner of Fahrdienst Schwabia to manage the entire website and booking platform without technical knowledge.

The dashboard works as a lightweight CMS (Content Management System).

The administrator must be able to control:

\- Bookings

\- Prices

\- Destinations

\- Website content

\- Images

\- SEO settings

\- Company information

\- Contact details

\- Social media links
